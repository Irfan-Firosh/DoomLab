## Improvement trial

- **Corrections:** 11 regression cases. I reviewed all 11 corrections: I wrote 2, and AI drafted the other 9 from the ViZDoom scenario docs.
- **Candidate v1:** 3 lessons proposed by an Analyst LLM from mined failure patterns.
- **Offline:** correction cases passed 3/11 (v0) → 7/11 (v1).
- **In play** (react_single, health_gathering_supreme, validation seeds): v1 survived shorter on 4 of 5 seeds.
- **Gate:** PROMOTE. **Human decision:** REJECT: "Passes 7/11 corrections and cut observe 32 to 1, but survival fell on 4 of 5 seeds (median -36%); it traded observe for back-and-forth turning. Gate promoted on one extra success."
